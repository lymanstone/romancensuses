      Territorial reach (edge blur)

        Shapes are schematic. Province outlines are hand-drawn approximations, clipped to modern coastlines. Their inland edges are not survey boundaries, and Roman provincial borders moved over time. Ager Romanus before 89 BC was discontinuous; it is drawn as one blurred block.

        Jurisdiction categories. Senatorial provinces were governed by proconsuls, so "senatorial" and "proconsular" are one category here. Imperial provinces are split into those run by a legate of Augustus and those run by an equestrian prefect or procurator (Egypt, Judaea). After 284 the senatorial/imperial split stops meaning much for census work, so Tetrarchic and later assessments get their own colour. Republican-era city censuses in Sicily and Bithynia are civic.

        Dates. Republican censuses are dated by year of election. Egyptian census years run from 29 August, so 33/34 means the census of the Egyptian year beginning in AD 33. Undated career inscriptions are given an assigned span (often the whole Principate) and the lowest evidence rating; switch them off under Include to see only datable operations. Several prefects' names and inscription concordances in the source catalogue were marked for verification; check Broughton's MRR, Bagnall & Frier, and Le Teuff (2012) before citing.
